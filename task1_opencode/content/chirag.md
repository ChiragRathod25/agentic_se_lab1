## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI tools make development much faster, especially for debugging, writing tests, and repetitive tasks. But they still struggle with understanding the bigger picture of complex projects. I see AI as a coding assistant, not a replacement for understanding what I'm building.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
AI agents will probably handle much of the routine coding, testing, and debugging. Software engineers will focus more on architecture, problem-solving, and coordinating AI systems. Understanding how to build and manage intelligent systems will become an important skill.

## My hot take on AI
AI won't replace good software engineers, but software engineers who know how to use AI effectively might replace those who don't.
